# poem_server_room (creative)

## Task

Write a 14-line poem about a server room at 3 a.m. Rhyme scheme ABAB CDCD EFEF GG. Use the word "lantern" once, and do not use the word "silence".

## What a good answer does

14 lines, ABAB CDCD EFEF GG, 'lantern' exactly once, no 'silence'. Judge craft and imagery too.

## Answers

### A

The air bites sharp with winter's frozen hold,
Where racks of servers stand in gray array,
And no human voice can answer when you call,
While information flows from night to day.

The blue-light burns with calm and steady glow,
As cooling vents exhale a bone-deep chill;
No shadows play, save where a lantern glows,
Where signals race to scale the digital hill.

The rest of world is lost in heavy sleep,
While copper veins drink deep the liquid gold;
No sun breaks through the dark and secret keep,
As circuits forge a path through winter's mold.

The midnight brings no pause to rack and wire,
As streams of light sustain the digital fire.

### B

The world sleeps deep while data streams unwind,
Cool air blows through the metal grid of racks;
No human breath disturbs this wired mind,
Just hums that echo round the server stacks.
A constellation forms of blinking lights,
Within this hall where shadows dare not creep;
The processor burns through the velvet nights,
And promises of waking worlds to keep.
A lone lantern casts its amber glow,
Upon the rows where fiber optics run;
It guards the pulse in this electric flow,
As daylight waits for when the night is done.
The hour turns to three, the logs roll on,
Until the dawn reclaims what night has spun.

### C

The constant hum provides a steady beat,
Amidst the racks of steel and glowing blue.
Where darkness and the metal shadows meet,
The data streams are pulsing, rushing through.

A low-pitched drone replaces evening rest,
As cables coil like serpents on the floor.
The cooling air is put to every test,
Behind the heavy, locked, and bolted door.

No candle flickers with a dancing flame,
Each tiny LED, a lantern bright.
The binary whispers a secret name,
Within the depth of this electric night.

At three a.m., the circuit's lonely song,
Is where the currents of the world belong.

### D

The server towers rise in endless lines,
The hum of fans is all the voice they make;
Where binary stars in fiber-optic twines,
To save the dreams that restless minds forsake.
A single lantern casts a wavering beam,
Upon the console where the metrics climb;
It cuts the dark within this frozen dream,
And measures out the ticking of the time.
The world below lies buried in the deep,
While circuits wake to process thoughts unseen;
Within these rows, no weary head doth sleep,
But sorts the data's vast and constant stream.
At three a.m. the digital gods awake,
And forge a kingdom that will never break.

### E

The server room at three glows faint and low,
The fans churn on with a restless, deep hum,
The cables coil in orderly rows,
As cool air pushes past and starts to drum.

The temperature gauge gives off a chill,
The status lights, a lantern, cut the night,
The quiet racks stand stock-still,
While every circuit glows bright.

The server rack pulls cooling air,
The processors spin in frantic flare,
The storage arrays draw in the air,
And backup batteries hum with care.

The watchdog keeps the power deep,
And server racks drift into sleep.

### F

The server room is wrapped in gloom
The fans at three a.m. begin to hum
Green lights blink through frost and gloom
And every backup starts to drum

The temperature begins to pulse
The gauges hold a sickly light
The cooling pumps maintain the pulse
And every rack survives the night

The network core begins to roar
The data streams run through the air
The firewall stands and asks for more
While midnight logs conspire in prayer

The little lantern flickers, low and blue,
And keeps the server shadows safe and true

### G

The cooling fans hum in a steady drone,
Blue lights are blinking in the velvet night.
Within this hall, I sit and feel alone,
While cables weave a web of neon light.
The heavy air is chilled by rapid flow,
A restless pulse within the metal rack.
A rhythmic glow begins to softly show,
Against the shadows deep and midnight black.
Each status light, a lantern in the gloom,
Conducts the data through the copper veins.
Within this cold and electronic womb,
The constant hum of processing remains.
The clock strikes three while all the city sleeps,
While this electric heart its vigil keeps.

### H

The hum of machines fills the darkened space,
While cities dream beneath the heavy moon;
No human breath disturbs this digital race,
Where blinking eyes track data in the gloom.

The endless fans keep time in soft refrain,
As shadows stretch across the server rack;
No lantern guides the eye through electric rain,
Until the dawn dissolves this steel and black.

The green LEDs wink like distant stars,
That never fade within this concrete grave;
While sleep-bound dreams drift past these metal bars,
They hold the future that the world might crave.

The world outside is wrapped in wool and sleep,
These humming cores awake their vigil keep.
