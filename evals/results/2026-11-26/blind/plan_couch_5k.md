Notice there is at least one day (preferably two) between your runs. Your muscles and tendons need time to repair the micro-tears caused by running. Do not run on consecutive days during these 6 weeks.

### 💡 Tips for Success
1.  **The "Talk Test":** You should be able to speak in full sentences while running. If you're gasping, slow down to a jog or walk until your breath recovers.
2.  **Consistency Over Speed:** Your goal is to finish without stopping, not how fast you do it. A slow jog is faster progress than burning out.
3.  **Rest is Training:** Muscle adaptation happens on rest days. Do not skip recovery days.
4.  **Gear:** Wear supportive running shoes. If you don't have them, consider visiting a running store for advice or using sturdy trainers.
5.  **Modification:** If Week 4 feels too hard, repeat Week 3 for an extra week. This is a marathon, not a sprint!
6.  **Post-Run:** Stretch your calves, hamstrings, and hips gently after every session to prevent tightness.

**You can do this.** Stick to the plan, respect your limits, and trust the process. Good luck! 🏃💨
