# math_trains (chat)

## Task

A train leaves a station at 9:40 travelling at 72 km/h. A second train leaves the same station at 10:15 on a parallel track in the same direction at 96 km/h. At what time does the second train draw level with the first, and how far from the station are they then?

## What a good answer does

12:00, 168 km from the station.

## Answers

### A

To find the time and distance at which the second train catches up to the first, we can follow these steps:

### 1. Determine the head start of the first train
The first train leaves at 9:40 and the second leaves at 10:15. We first calculate the time difference:
*   From 9:40 to 10:00 is **20 minutes**.
*   From 10:00 to 10:15 is **15 minutes**.
*   **Total head start time** = 35 minutes.

Since the speeds are in km/h, we convert the time into hours:
$$\text{Time} = \frac{35}{60} \text{ hours} = \frac{7}{12} \text{ hours}$$

Now, calculate how far the first train has traveled during this head start:
$$\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$$
$$\text{Distance} = 72 \text{ km/h} \times \frac{7}{12} \text{ h} = 6 \times 7 = \mathbf{42 \text{ km}}$$

### 2. Calculate the relative speed
Since both trains are traveling in the same direction, the second train is "closing the gap" at a rate equal to the difference in their speeds:
$$\text{Relative Speed} = 96 \text{ km/h} - 72 \text{ km/h} = \mathbf{24 \text{ km/h}}$$

### 3. Calculate the time required to catch up
To find how long it takes the second train to close the 42 km gap:
$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Gap Distance}}{\text{Relative Speed}}$$
$$\text{Time} = \frac{42 \text{ km}}{24 \text{ km/h}} = 1.75 \text{ hours}$$
Converting 0.75 hours to minutes: $0.75 \times 60 = 45 \text{ minutes}$. 
So, the second train catches the first after **1 hour and 45 minutes** of traveling.

### 4. Find the meeting time and distance
**The Time:**
The second train started at 10:15.
$$10:15 + 1 \text{ hour } 45 \text{ minutes} = \mathbf{12:00 \text{ (Noon)}}$$

**The Distance:**
We calculate the distance covered by the second train in 1.75 hours:
$$\text{Distance} = 96 \text{ km/h} \times 1.75 \text{ h} = \mathbf{168 \text{ km}}$$
*(Verification using Train 1: Train 1 traveled for 2 hours and 20 minutes total, or $7/3$ hours. $72 \times 7/3 = 168$ km. It matches.)*

**Final Answer:**
The second train will draw level with the first at **12:00 (Noon)**, at a distance of **168 km** from the station.
