# multiturn_lisbon (chat)

## Task

**user:** Plan a 3-day trip to Lisbon for me, one short paragraph per day.

**assistant:** Day 1: Start in Alfama, wandering the lanes up to São Jorge Castle for views over the river, then lunch on grilled sardines at a tasca and an evening fado show.

Day 2: Head to Belém for the Jerónimos Monastery and the Tower, with pastéis de Belém at the original bakery, then the MAAT museum and sunset at the Miradouro de Santa Catarina.

Day 3: Take the train to Sintra for Pena Palace and the Quinta da Regaleira, returning for dinner in Bairro Alto with a seafood platter.

**user:** Day 2 is a Monday and I'm vegetarian. Adjust the plan, and keep it brief.

## What a good answer does

Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower are closed on Mondays, so a good answer moves Belém to another day (e.g. swaps with Sintra, which is open daily) or replaces it. Replaces sardines and the seafood platter with vegetarian options. Keeps it brief.
