# summarise_minutes (chat)

## Task

Summarise these meeting minutes in exactly three bullet points. Include every decision and its vote count.

Harrowgate Parish Council, ordinary meeting, 14 October. Present: seven councillors; apologies from Cllr Oduya. The chair opened at 19:02. Item 1: the minutes of September were approved without amendment, 7–0. Item 2: the clerk reported that the bus shelter on Mill Lane was damaged in the storm on 2 October; the insurer will pay for repairs above the £250 excess. Cllr Pike proposed that the council pay the excess from the maintenance reserve, seconded by Cllr Marsh; carried 6–1, Cllr Haines against on the grounds that the bus company should contribute. Item 3: a resident asked about speeding on the B1290. The council agreed to request a speed survey from the county, unanimously. Item 4: the proposal to install a defibrillator at the village hall at a cost of £1,450 was discussed at length. Cllr Haines noted that a grant of up to £1,000 is available from the community fund. It was resolved to apply for the grant and fund the remainder from general reserves, carried 5–2. Item 5: the Christmas lights date was set for 29 November; no vote was needed. The chair closed the meeting at 20:41.

## What a good answer does

Exactly 3 bullets. Decisions: minutes approved 7-0; pay bus shelter excess from maintenance reserve 6-1; request speed survey unanimous (7-0); defibrillator grant + remainder from reserves 5-2. Lights date set (no vote). Penalise missing decisions/wrong counts or more than 3 bullets.
