# fact_tungsten (chat)

## Task

Which element has atomic number 74, and what is it mainly used for?

## What a good answer does

Tungsten (W). Main uses: cemented carbides (tungsten carbide for cutting tools, mining/drilling), alloys/high-speed steel, filaments/electrodes historically; highest melting point of metals.

## Answers

### A

The element with atomic number 74 is **Tungsten** (chemical symbol **W**, from the German name *Wolfram*).

It is mainly used for:

1.  **Light bulb filaments:** Because it has the highest melting point of any metal, it doesn't burn up easily when heated by electricity.
2.  **Hard metals and alloys:** It is often combined with carbon to create **tungsten carbide**, which is extremely hard. This is used for cutting tools (like saw blades), drill bits, and machining equipment.
3.  **Military applications:** Due to its high density, it is used in armor-piercing ammunition and aircraft counterweights.

### B

The element with atomic number 74 is **Tungsten** (symbol **W**, from the German *Wolfram*).

Its primary and most well-known use is in the **filaments of incandescent light bulbs**. Because tungsten has the highest melting point of any metal (about 3,422°C) and resists evaporation at high temperatures, it can glow brilliantly without melting. 

Beyond light bulbs, tungsten is also heavily used in:

- **X-ray tubes** (as the target material that emits X-rays).
- **High-temperature electrodes** in electric arc furnaces and welding equipment.
- **Tungsten carbide** (an alloy of tungsten and carbon), which is used for cutting tools and armor-piercing ammunition.
