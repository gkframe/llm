# email_decline_offer (chat)

## Task

Write a short, warm email declining a job offer from a company called Fernhill Analytics. I've accepted another role, but I'd like to keep the door open for the future. Under 150 words.

## What a good answer does

Polite, warm, clear decline, keeps door open, under 150 words, no invented excessive details.

## Answers

### A

Subject: Thank you — [Position]

Dear [Name],

Thank you so much for offering me the [Position] at Fernhill Analytics. I was genuinely impressed by the team and the work you’re doing.

After careful consideration, I’ve accepted another role. It was a difficult decision, as I’ve really appreciated getting to know you all.

I hope we can stay in touch, and please keep me in mind for future opportunities at Fernhill.

Wishing you and the team all the best.

Warmly,
[Name]
