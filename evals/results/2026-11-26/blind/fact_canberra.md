## Task

What's the capital of Australia, and why isn't it Sydney?

## What a good answer does

Canberra. Chosen as a compromise between rival Sydney and Melbourne (constitution required a site in NSW at least 100 miles from Sydney); Melbourne was temporary seat until 1927.

## Answers

### A

The capital of Australia is **Canberra**.

It's not Sydney mainly due to a compromise. When Australia federated in 1901, Sydney and Melbourne—the two largest cities and biggest rivals—fought bitterly over which should become the capital. Neither would agree to the other, so they struck a deal: a new city would be built somewhere between the two.

Canberra was chosen through an international design competition won by American architect Walter Burley Griffin in 1913, and it officially became the seat of government in 1927.

The rivalry continued for decades, with Sydney and Melbourne both lobbying against Canberra's selection, but it ultimately stuck. Today, Canberra remains a purpose-built capital, while Sydney and Melbourne continue to compete as the country's biggest cities.

The capital of Australia is **Canberra**.

The reason it isn't Sydney (or Melbourne) is the result of a long-standing political compromise. Here is the breakdown of why that happened:

### 1. The Rivalry
During the late 19th century, as the six British colonies were working toward becoming a single nation (Federation), there was intense rivalry between the two largest cities: **Sydney** and **Melbourne**. 

Sydney wanted to be the capital because it was the oldest and most established colony, while Melbourne argued it was the wealthiest and most populous at the time. Neither side was willing to let the other have the honor.

### 2. The Compromise
To prevent the political deadlock from tearing the new federation apart, a compromise was written into the **Australian Constitution** in 1901. It stated that:
*   The capital must be located within the state of New South Wales (to satisfy Sydney).
*   However, it could not be in the city of Sydney itself.
*   It also had to be at least 100 miles (160 km) away from either Sydney or Melbourne (to satisfy Melbourne).

### 3. The Creation of a "Planned City"
Because of this compromise, a brand-new city had to be built from scratch in a neutral area. A site in the Yass-Canberra region was selected in 1908. 

Unlike Sydney, which grew organically around a harbor, Canberra is a **"planned city."** It was designed by American architects Walter Burley Griffin and Marion Mahony Griffin, who used a geometric pattern of circles, triangles, and hexagons.

### Summary
While Sydney is Australia's largest and most famous city, and Melbourne is its cultural and sporting hub, **Canberra** was created specifically to serve as a neutral ground to keep the peace between the two major cities.
